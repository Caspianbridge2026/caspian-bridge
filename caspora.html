The brand

Protection for every moving part.

From the piston crown to the crankshaft and the gearbox, a Caspora film sits between every metal surface — cutting friction, carrying away heat, and holding its viscosity when the temperature climbs and the load doesn't let up. Blended in Türkiye, quality-assured from Britain.
