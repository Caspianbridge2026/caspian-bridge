How It Works

Our Process — From Inquiry to Delivery

Our structured five-step process ensures every procurement is handled with precision, accountability and full commercial transparency — from the first conversation to final delivery and documentation.

1
Inquiry & Scoping
You share your requirements — product specs, quantities, destination, timeline and budget. We provide an initial assessment within 24 hours.
2
Supplier Identification
We search our verified network and wider European market to identify qualified, compliant suppliers who meet your technical and commercial specifications.
3
Negotiation & Due Diligence
We negotiate commercial terms, verify supplier credentials, inspect documentation and where required arrange factory visits or third-party audits.
4
Contract & Compliance
Formal commercial agreements are drafted, export licences obtained where required, banking and payment structures arranged, and compliance verified.
5
Logistics & Delivery
We coordinate freight, customs clearance, shipping documentation and final delivery — with full tracking and reporting at every stage.

Who We Are

A Global Commercial Bridge

Caspian Bridge International Ltd is a UK-registered private limited company established to serve as a trusted commercial bridge between suppliers and buyers across the globe — with no geographic limits on where we source or where we supply.

We operate at the intersection of global commercial expertise and the growth ambitions of businesses in every market. Our model is simple: we bring the credibility, compliance and supplier access of a UK-based entity — combined with the flexibility and reach to trade in any country, across any sector.

Our Commitments

Operating with Full Transparency

Caspian Bridge International Ltd is committed to the highest standards of legal compliance, ethical business practice and transparent commercial conduct. Every engagement we undertake is governed by formal written agreements and conducted within applicable UK and international regulatory frameworks.

🔒
Privacy & Data Protection
We are fully committed to protecting personal and commercial data in accordance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. All client and counterparty data is handled with the highest level of confidentiality.
  • Personal data collected only for lawful commercial purposes
  • Data is never sold or disclosed to unauthorised third parties
  • All data is stored securely and processed lawfully
  • You may request access, correction or deletion at any time
  • Data breach notification procedures are in place
  • Contact: info@caspibridge.com
📋
Terms of Business
All commercial engagements with Caspian Bridge International Ltd are governed by formal written agreements — agreed and signed prior to any commercial activity commencing.
  • All engagement terms agreed in writing before commencement
  • Fee structures, commission rates and payment terms fully disclosed upfront
  • English law governs all contracts unless specifically agreed otherwise
  • Disputes subject to English courts or agreed arbitration
  • Limitation of liability provisions apply to all engagements
  • Termination clauses clearly defined in all agreements
⚖️
Trade Compliance & Export Controls
We operate in strict compliance with UK export control legislation, HM Revenue & Customs regulations and applicable international sanctions regimes including UN, EU and OFSI sanctions.
  • Full compliance with the Export Control Order 2008 and amendments
  • HMRC registered for international trade activities (EORI)
  • All transactions screened against applicable sanctions lists
  • Export licences obtained where legally required
  • No facilitation of transactions involving sanctioned entities or goods
  • Dual-use goods handled in accordance with UK controls
🤝
Anti-Bribery & Ethics
We maintain zero tolerance for bribery, corruption and unethical commercial conduct — fully compliant with the UK Bribery Act 2010 and international anti-corruption standards.
  • Zero tolerance for bribery, corruption or facilitation payments
  • All commercial relationships conducted at arm's length
  • No gifts or hospitality beyond de minimis thresholds accepted
  • Conflicts of interest disclosed immediately and managed appropriately
  • Whistleblowing procedures available to all staff and counterparties
  • Anti-money laundering (AML) procedures applied to all engagements

Common Questions
What is your typical response time?
We respond to all email and WhatsApp inquiries within one business day. For urgent requirements, WhatsApp usually gets the fastest response from our team.
What is the minimum transaction size?
We work with transactions from $10,000 upwards. For very small transactions, our success fee model provides the most appropriate structure. Larger projects ($500K+) are typically better suited to our retainer or management fee models.
Do you work with Iran?
We facilitate trade involving Iran only for goods that are legally permissible under UK and international sanctions frameworks. All such transactions are carefully screened and the appropriate export licensing is obtained where required.
Can you arrange factory inspections?
Yes — we coordinate factory visits, third-party quality inspections and product testing on behalf of our clients. We work with recognised inspection bodies including SGS, Bureau Veritas and others as required.
