About Caspian Bridge

Who We Are

A Trusted Commercial Bridge

Caspian Bridge International Ltd is a United Kingdom registered company focused on international trade, procurement, sourcing and strategic business consultancy.

Our mission is to create reliable commercial bridges between manufacturers, suppliers and buyers across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and worldwide — providing access to global markets through a trusted, UK-based commercial framework.

We operate across four core sectors — Energy & Petrochemicals, Industrial Equipment, Agriculture & Livestock, and Consumer Products — combining deep product knowledge, an extensive supplier network and rigorous commercial standards.

UK Legal Status
Caspian Bridge International Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales. All engagements are conducted under formal written agreements governed by English law.
